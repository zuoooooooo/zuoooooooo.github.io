I'm a PhD student in Statistics & Data Science at the [Wharton School](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/) of the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). Previously, I studied statistics at the [School of the Gifted Young](https://en.scgy.ustc.edu.cn/), [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/).

I am interested in learning theory, including the theoretical understanding of diffusion models and Transformers, optimization theory, and high-dimensional statistics. I am also particularly interested in post-training for large language models.

During my undergraduate studies, I had the great pleasure of working with Prof. [Dacheng Xiu](https://dachxiu.chicagobooth.edu/) and Prof. [Weijing Tang](https://sites.google.com/andrew.cmu.edu/weijingtang/home).

If you are interested in my research or would like to discuss some interesting topics with me, please feel free to contact me!
